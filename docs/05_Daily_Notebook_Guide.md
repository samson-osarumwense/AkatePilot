# 05 · What to Write in the Notebook Every Day

**Rule:** if it is not in the notebook, it does not exist. Use one hard-cover exercise book — **one page per day**, with the date at the top. Rule the page into the sections below. Type the page into AkatePilot every evening, then tick it "typed".

## Page layout (copy this onto each page)
```
DATE: ______  DAY: ____  ON DUTY: ________   OPENING CASH: ₦______

A. SALES                                                         
 Product | R/WH/WF | Qty | Paid by (C/T/P/CR) | Customer (credit/regular only) | Special price
 ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 (one line per product sold)                         R = retail  WH = wholesale half  WF = wholesale full
                                                     C = cash  T = transfer  P = POS card  CR = credit
 SACHET WATER BAGS FINISHED:  | | | | | |   (one tick per BAG, "½" for half)

B. STOCK IN (arrived today)        Product | Qty | Unit (crate/carton/pack/piece) | Price for ONE unit | Supplier
C. BROKEN / LEAKED / EXPIRED / FAMILY USE   Product | Qty | What happened | Who
D. MONEY OUT                       What | ₦ | (transport, repairs, airtime, POS charge ...)   CASH TAKEN HOME: ₦____
E. DEBT PAID BACK TODAY            Customer | ₦ | Cash/Transfer

CLOSING:  Cash in hand ₦____  Transfers ₦____  POS ₦____  Credit given ₦____  = TOTAL SALES ₦____
FAST-SELLER COUNT LEFT:  Coke __  Fanta __  Heineken __  Goldberg __  33 Export __  Sachet water __ bags
INITIALS: ____   TYPED INTO AKATEPILOT: ☐
```

## The 17 things, in order
| When | Write |
|---|---|
| **Morning (2 min)** | 1 Date, day, who is on duty · 2 Cash in the till (float) · 3 "LOW:" next to any fast seller nearly finished |
| **Every sale** | 4 Product · R/WH/WF · quantity · paid by · customer name *only* for credit or regular customers · price only if not the normal price |
| **Sachet water** | 5 One tick per **bag** finished from the cooler (3 bags are loaded at a time) |
| **Stock arrives** | 6 Product · quantity · unit · price of **one** unit · supplier · plus transport/offloading money |
| **Breakage, leakage, family use** | 7 Product · quantity · what happened · who |
| **Money out** | 8 Every expense · 9 Cash taken home · 10 Credit repayments received (who, how much) |
| **Closing (5 min)** | 11 Count cash, check transfers in the bank app, POS total, credit given · 12 Write the day's total · 13 Count the 6 fast sellers · 14 Initial the page |
| **Evening (10 min)** | 15 Type the page into SALES, STOCK IN-OUT, EXPENSES, CREDIT · 16 Compare your total with the *END-OF-DAY TOTALS* line on top of SALES — find any missing line · 17 Tick "typed" |

## Once-only pages (front of the book)
* **Product page** — name, how it is bought (crate/carton/pack/bag), pieces per pack, cost of one pack, retail price of one piece, price of one full pack, reorder level. Update with the date when a price changes.
* **Customer page** — name, phone, area, credit limit, what they usually buy.
* **Supplier page** — name, phone, delivery days, what they supply.

## Weekly (Sunday morning, before opening)
Count the shelves with a second person: full packs, then loose pieces → write *packs × pieces per pack + loose = total pieces*, type it in **STOCK → Physical Count**. Fast sellers weekly; slow sellers and spirits monthly. If the difference is more than 2 pieces, recount before recording a loss.

## Why each item matters
Sales lines → profit and best sellers · special prices → real margin · customer names (credit only) → who owes you · stock-in with unit price → true cost and supplier price changes · breakage/family use → hidden losses · money out → net profit · closing cash check → catches missing sales or theft early · fast-seller count → proves the stock system matches the shelf.
